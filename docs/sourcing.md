# Sourcing

## Decision

**HDX 27-gal Tough Tote, black/yellow, from Home Depot.** $8.98 at the Port Huron (Fort Gratiot) store on October 11, 2026. Every rack is built around this one tote.

Reasons: closest store, sold singly, no membership, frequent sales to $6.97.

## Why one brand only

The common 27-gallon totes are not the same size, so a rack cut for one brand won't fit another.

- **HDX** (Home Depot, made by Edge Plastics) is slightly taller and shorter in length than Greenmade and Commander.
- **Greenmade** (Costco, Office Depot, Staples) and **Commander** (Lowe's) stack with each other.
- HDX can stack on Greenmade/Commander, but not the other way around.

Customers who bring their own totes: check the brand. If they're not HDX, measure under the lip and set the rail gap in the calculator before quoting.
