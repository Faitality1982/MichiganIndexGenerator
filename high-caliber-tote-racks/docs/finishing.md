# Finishing

## Options and pricing

| Finish | Customer price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Raw wood | $0 | Kiln-dried pine as bought. Fine for a garage; yellows and picks up dirt over time. |
| Clear seal | $60 + $5/slot | One coat of water-based polyurethane on every board. Keeps the wood look, wipes clean. |
| Stain + seal | $90 + $9/slot | Stain on frames and stretchers, then clear coat on everything. |
| Paint | $100 + $10/slot | Two coats on frames and stretchers. Rails get clear coat only. |

Examples: 12-slot rack paint = $220, 30-slot rack paint = $400.

These pay about $45 to $50/hr on small racks and $60 to $70/hr on big ones at the default labor estimates (3 minutes per board per coat, plus 30 minutes setup per coat).

## Rules

- **Finish boards before assembly.** Rolling flat boards is far faster than brushing around 60 rails on a built rack.
- **Rails get clear coat only.** Tote lips slide on the rails and will scrape paint off within weeks.
- **Cold weather.** Latex paint and water-based poly need roughly 50°F or warmer to cure. October through April, finishing needs a heated space and adds 1 to 2 days of lead time. Raw racks are unaffected.
- **Sell clear seal as the standard upgrade.** Low price for the customer, good return per hour.

## Material estimates (unverified)

The calculator figures finish materials from board surface area: a 2x4 has a 10" perimeter, so an 8-ft board is about 6.7 sq ft.

- Coverage on raw pine: 250 sq ft per gallon per coat (assumption; raw wood soaks up more than the can says)
- Paint: about $40/gallon
- Stain: about $40/gallon
- Water-based poly: about $50/gallon
- Rollers, brushes, rags: $15 per job

A 30-slot wall rack is about 241 sq ft of lumber. Painting it takes about 1.25 gallons of paint plus 0.5 gallon of clear for the rails, roughly $78 in materials.
