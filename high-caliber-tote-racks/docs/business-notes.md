# Business notes

## Who buys this

Estate sale clients are getting rid of things, so they usually don't need storage. The likely buyers:

- **Heirs keeping items** from an estate and needing somewhere to put them
- **Downsizers** moving into a smaller house or condo with a garage or basement
- **Garage cleanout customers**, during or after a cleanout
- **Holiday decor storage** in fall and after the holidays

## How to offer it

- Mention it at walkthroughs and pickups: "We also build storage racks for the stuff you keep."
- Bring a printed quote example and a photo of a finished rack once the first one is built.
- Mobile racks can be built in the home shop between sales and delivered. Wall racks need a site visit to measure.

## Questions to settle before the first paid job

- **Insurance.** Confirm the general liability policy covers carpentry and installation work in customers' homes, including anchoring to walls.
- **Sales tax on totes.** Totes are resold goods. Ask the accountant whether to collect Michigan sales tax on them. The calculator has a "Sales tax on totes" setting (default 0%).
- **Deposit and terms.** Default is a 50% deposit with the balance due on delivery or install. Confirm with Casey.
- **Contact info on quotes.** Phone and email are blank in this copy. Fill them in under Pricing & cost settings, or set them in the DEFAULTS block in calculator/index.html.
