# Build spec

Designed around the **HDX 27-gal Tough Tote** (Home Depot). Totes hang by their lip on 2x4 rails and slide in like drawers.

## Tote dimensions (manufacturer spec)

- Exterior at top: 28.55" L x 19.61" W x 15.27" H
- Interior at bottom: 23.5" L x 14.5" W x 13" H
- Weight capacity 75 lb

**Measure one before cutting.** The rail gap must be the tote's width just under the lip plus 1/4". That measurement is not published. The calculator defaults to 19" and warns on every quote.

## How the rack is built

- **Frames ("ladders")**: one frame between each column of totes, plus one at each end. A frame is two 2x4 uprights (front and back) with 27" 2x4 rails screwed across them, one rail per row on each face. End frames have rails on the inside face only.
- **Rails**: 2x4 on edge, 27" long, running front to back. The tote lip rests on top.
- **Stretchers**: four long 2x4s tie the frames together: top front, top back, bottom front, bottom back. On wall units the top back stretcher is screwed into wall studs.
- **Mobile base**: one 27" cross piece under each frame, locking casters, and diagonal 2x4 braces across the back.

Totes stick out about 1-1/2" past the front.

## Geometry (all inches)

| Value | Formula | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Rail gap G | under-lip width + 1/4 | 19 |
| Row spacing P | tote height + lid clearance | 17 |
| Rail length / depth D | | 27 |
| Upright length U | H x P + 6 | 5 high = 91 |
| Overall width | W x G + (W - 1) x 4.5 + 6 | 6 wide = 142-1/2 |
| Mobile height | U + 5-1/2 | 4 high = 79-1/2 |

Interior frames are 4-1/2" thick (rail + upright + rail). End frames are 3".

## Lumber takeoff

- Uprights: 2 x (W + 1). Cut from 8 ft if U is 96 or less, otherwise 10 ft.
- Rails: 2 x W x H at 27". Three per 8-ft board. Upright offcuts get used when they're 27-1/8" or longer.
- Mobile base cross pieces: W + 1 at 27", from the same boards as rails.
- Stretchers: 4 at the overall width. 8, 10, 12, or 16 ft board; longer than 16 ft gets spliced over a frame.
- Mobile back braces: 1 per 2 columns.
- Casters: 4 (1-2 wide), 6 (3-4 wide), 8 (5+ wide).

## Limits and warnings the calculator checks

- Mobile racks over 80" tall won't roll upright through a standard door.
- Mobile racks wider than 4 columns get heavy and wobbly; recommend a wall unit.
- Wall uprights over 94" need a ceiling check so frames can be tipped up.
- Stretchers over 16 ft get spliced; stagger splices front and back.

## Build flow

1. Measure the tote under the lip. Set the rail gap.
2. Cut everything at home. Label frames.
3. If finishing, finish boards before assembly (see `finishing.md`).
4. Assemble frames at home (mobile: assemble the whole rack).
5. On site: stand frames, screw on stretchers, check level and square, find studs, anchor the top back stretcher.
6. Slide in totes, check every slot.
