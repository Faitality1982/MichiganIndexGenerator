# Pricing

All figures are estimates set on October 11, 2026. Lumber and paint prices were not checked at the Port Huron (Fort Gratiot) Home Depot. See `open-questions.md`.

Price per rack, not per hour. The customer sees a flat number; the shop sheet tracks whether it paid.

## Why these numbers

- **$395 start.** Covers lumber, screws, and roughly 5 to 6 hours for a 12-slot rack with a profit near $55/hr. Small jobs need a floor because setup, the Home Depot run, and travel cost the same no matter the size.
- **$20 per extra slot.** Each slot adds two rails (about 2/3 of a 2x4, roughly $2.70), screws, and about 9 minutes of labor. $20 keeps big racks paying better per hour than small ones.
- **$75 mobile.** Six casters run about $60 (unverified), plus base cross pieces and back braces. Mobile racks are built in the shop and delivered, so no wall anchoring time.
- **$14 per tote.** HDX 27-gal is $8.98 at the Fort Gratiot store (screenshot, October 11, 2026). With 6% tax that's about $9.52. The markup covers buying, hauling, and the risk of a cracked lid.
- **Finish: setup + per slot.** A flat per-slot rate underpays small racks because cleanup time is fixed. See `finishing.md`.

## Comparison points found online

- One cost guide put a single 4-ft overhead garage rack install at $470 total ($180 materials, 2 hours at $85/hr). Source: overhead garage storage installation cost guide 2026 (risingsunartscentre.org mirror; low-quality source, use only as a rough sanity check).
- A free DIY plan for a 15-tote rack estimates $120 to $150 in materials and 8 to 10 hours for a homeowner (howtospecialist.com). Our crew with pre-cut lumber should beat that time.
- Etsy sells the plans alone for about $8 to $11 (MyJewelyCrafts listing seen October 11, 2026). Customers who would buy plans are DIYers, not our buyer.
